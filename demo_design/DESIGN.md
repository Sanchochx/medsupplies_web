## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies rigorous biomedical engineering, regulatory reliability, and institutional trust for hospital networks, clinical procurement teams, and biomedical departments. The aesthetic merges corporate biomedical professionalism with modern technological sophistication, evoking composure, sterile precision, and uncompromising dependability.

The design relies on a structured **Corporate Minimalist** foundation elevated with **High-Tech Clinical** cues:
- Uncluttered, generous spatial architecture with dominant clinical white backdrops.
- Razor-sharp line iconography and structural compartmentalization that reflects engineering discipline.
- Deep, stable navy surfaces providing gravity and authority against light, airy clinical canvases.
- Restraint in visual noise: zero decorative gradients, no unnecessary ornamental color pops, and an emphasis on technical hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated around institutional trust and sterile clarity, eliminating extraneous decorative hues to align with medical-grade precision.

- **Primary (`#0D6EFD`)**: Vibrant clinical cobalt utilized for primary interactive states, actionable targets, focus rings, and high-priority status indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0A58CA`)**: Deep clinical azure used for hover transitions, active states, key interactive headers, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary / Structural Navy (`#0B192C`)**: Deep, authoritative navy reserved for high-impact grounding elements: corporate hero blocks, persistent sidebars, technical specification headers, and footer anchors.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure medical white dominating the viewport to establish an open, sterile foundation.
- **Subtle Surface Tint (`#F8FAFC`)**: Soft slate-white for grouped card backings, data-table alt rows, and filter sidebars.
- **Structural Borders (`#E2E8F0`)**: Crisp, low-contrast neutral divider gray that defines boundaries without visual aggression.
- **Text & Semantics**: Primary typography is grounded in ultra-dense slate-black (`#111827`) to guarantee absolute accessibility and readability across high-detail medical spec sheets. Secondary metadata, timestamps, and captions utilize muted slate (`#64748B`).

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headings with **Inter** for dense transactional interfaces, spec sheets, and catalog tables.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** lends an architectural, contemporary posture to titles and section headings. Its geometry delivers approachability without compromising clinical seriousness.
- **Inter** ensures optimum legibility at micro and regular scales for regulatory data, lot numbers, SKU references, and clinical guidelines.
- Heading scales feature tight letter tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) to emphasize precision and engineering confidence.
- Labels and micro-metadata apply slight positive tracking for fast scannability during procurement and inventory audits.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with strict vertical and horizontal cadence:
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 3rem outer page margins. Maximum container width caps at 1320px for optimal line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, and 2rem outer margins. Cards collapse from 3 or 4 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutters, and 1.25rem outer margins. Grids reflow strictly into single-column vertical stacks.

Spacing relies on an arithmetic 4px / 8px baseline rhythm. Generous vertical whitespace separates functional modules (e.g., technical capabilities, product catalogs, certification badges) to reflect medical room cleanliness and intellectual clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **subtle surface layering combined with low-contrast, hairline borders** and soft ambient light casting. Excessive drop shadows and high-contrast bevels are avoided.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` or alternate `#F8FAFC` backgrounds without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting)**: Framed with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-diffuse ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces)**: Elevated components (e.g., clickable medical device cards) transition subtly on hover: border shifts to `#0D6EFD` at 30% opacity, accompanied by `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Flyout Drawers)**: Floating clinical inspect sheets and quotation carts use a refined, focused shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`, backdropped by a deep navy tint (`#0B192C` at 40% opacity).

## Shapes

The design uses a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2`), balancing human-centered medical approachability with structural engineering rigor:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges)**: 0.5rem (`8px`) border radius. Provides soft tactile targets while maintaining strict geometric edges.
- **Cards & Data Panels (`rounded-lg`)**: 1.0rem (`16px`) border radius, framing clinical content and telemetry modules cleanly.
- **Feature Banners & Modals (`rounded-xl`)**: 1.5rem (`24px`) border radius for high-visibility marketing sections and dialogue overlays.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Small status pills and regulatory qualification chips use fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`) to immediately visually differentiate tags from structural containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0D6EFD` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, `label-lg` font weight. Interactive hover state shifts to `#0A58CA` with an ambient glow; active state compresses slightly (`scale-98`). Focus rings use a 2px offset with `#0D6EFD`.
- **Secondary / Engineering**: Ghost style with 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#111827` text, and `#FFFFFF` background. Hovers to `#F8FAFC` surface with `#0A58CA` border tint.
- **Tertiary / Institutional**: Solid `#0B192C` background with crisp white text for high-contrast B2B quotation triggers.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and `0.5rem` radius.
- Height standards: Default 44px (touch-compliant for mobile and tablet audits).
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`. Focused state transitions to a crisp `#0D6EFD` border accompanied by an inner ring of `rgba(13, 110, 253, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Data Containers
- Resting state features `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#E2E8F0` perimeter border, and `rounded-lg` (16px) curvature.
- Header sections inside cards utilize a subtle `#F8FAFC` bottom divider to segment specifications and product previews from operational actions.

### Badges & Regulatory Chips
- Monochromatic or low-saturation clinical tones: `#F8FAFC` background with `#0A58CA` text and an inline 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Fully rounded (`9999px`), padding: `0.25rem 0.75rem`, rendered in `label-md`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with 1.5px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Selected state fills with `#0D6EFD` displaying a crisp white 1.5px checkmark/dot icon.

### Specialized Components
- **Technical Specification Table**: Alternating rows of `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`, dense row paddings (`space-sm` vertical), and hairline horizontal rules for high-density medical equipment compatibility data.
- **Verification Stamp / Badge**: A dual-ringed micro-module communicating INVIMA regulatory compliance and ISO standards using crisp `#0A58CA` stroke styling.
